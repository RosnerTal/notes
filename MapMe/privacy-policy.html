Privacy Policy

Application: MapMe (com.talapp.mapme)
Effective Date: September 19, 2026 | Last Updated: September 19, 2026

Welcome to MapMe ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Tal Rosner. MapMe is an exploration tracker and navigation application designed for Android smartphones and Android Auto. It enables users to color every street in their city as they walk, run, or drive, and provides turn-by-turn navigation.

Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is handled, and your choices regarding your personal data when using the MapMe mobile app, Android Auto service, and accompanying web dashboard.

1. Prominent Disclosure: Location Data & Background Tracking

Important Location Notice (Google Play Compliance):
MapMe collects location data (including precise GPS coordinates, altitude, bearing, and speed) to enable:
  • Continuous Track Recording: Mapping and coloring streets you explore in real-time, even when the app is closed or running in the background while your phone is locked or you are using another app.
  • Turn-by-Turn Navigation: Guiding your walking and driving routes with real-time distance countdowns, audio cues, and off-route recalculation.
  • Android Auto In-Car Display: Projecting your active route and navigation maneuvers onto your vehicle's dashboard screen.
We NEVER sell, monetize, or share your location data with advertising networks or data brokers.

Background location access is only active when you explicitly tap "Start Walk" or "Start Drive", or begin turn-by-turn navigation. When background tracking is active, a persistent Android system notification is displayed with elapsed time, distance, and immediate pause/stop controls. Background location tracking ceases immediately the moment you stop the walk/drive session.

2. Information We Collect

A. Location Information

  • Precise Coordinates (GPS): Latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, speed, and timestamps recorded during active tracking sessions.
  • Destination Inquiries: Search queries entered when searching for points of interest or navigation targets (geocoded through OpenStreetMap Photon).

B. Account & Authentication Data

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a unique Firebase User ID (UID).
  • Guest / Anonymous Login: If you use Guest mode, a temporary anonymous identifier is assigned to preserve your tracks locally without requiring personal credentials.

C. User-Generated Exploration Content

  • Track Records: Total distance, duration, average speed, date/time, travel mode (Walking or Driving), and coordinate breadcrumbs.
  • Points of Interest (POIs): Custom titles, notes, and photos you voluntarily take or select from your device to attach to specific coordinates along your route.

D. Device & System Permissions

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: To determine accurate GPS location.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION: To allow uninterrupted track recording and navigation when the screen turns off.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): To display the required foreground service notification with recording controls.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To download OpenStreetMap map tiles, fetch OSRM navigation routes, and synchronize tracks with your cloud account.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provide and improve MapMe features:

  • To render your personal explored routes and city coverage heatmaps.
  • To compute turn-by-turn pedestrian and driving directions via open routing engines.
  • To sync your tracks securely between your mobile phone and the MapMe Web Dashboard.
  • To generate exportable GPX and KML files upon your request.
  • To prevent lost data by backing up your walking/driving achievements to your personal Firebase cloud profile.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

MapMe integrates with trusted third-party services that process minimal data solely to provide app functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Used for secure user authentication and cloud synchronization of your personal routes and POIs. Firebase stores your data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap (OSMDroid) & Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM): Used to render street map tiles and compute driving/walking paths. No personal account information is sent to map tile servers.
  • Photon Geocoding API (Komoot / OpenStreetMap): Used to search destinations and addresses. Queries may include approximate latitude and longitude to prioritize nearby results.

5. Data Storage, Security & Protection

We employ industry-standard security safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All network transmissions between the app, Firebase, and map services use encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Cloud database access is protected by strict Firebase Security Rules, ensuring that each user can only read and write their own tracks and POIs.
  • Local app databases on your device are stored within the Android sandboxed application directory, isolated from other apps.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

Full Control Over Your Data:
You can delete any track or point of interest directly within the app at any time.
To request complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud data, visit our dedicated Data Deletion Instructions Page or email talrosner@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

MapMe is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, displaying an in-app notice or website update.
