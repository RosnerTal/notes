Account & Data Deletion

Application: MapMe (com.talapp.mapme)
Developer: Tal Rosner | Compliance: Google Play Data Safety Policy

At MapMe, you have complete ownership and control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Data Safety requirements, this page provides instructions on how you can delete individual records or request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data.

1. How to Delete Your Data

You can delete your data using either of the two methods below:

Option A: In-App Track Deletion (Immediate)

If you only want to remove specific exploration tracks or clear local history without deleting your entire account:

  1. Open the MapMe app on your Android device.
  2. On the Dashboard, scroll to the Travel History list.
  3. Tap on the trip you wish to remove to open its Detail View.
  4. Tap the Delete Track icon (trash can) in the upper-right corner and confirm deletion.
  5. The track, its coordinates, distance metrics, and associated points of interest will be deleted immediately from both your local device and cloud sync.

Option B: Complete Account & Cloud Data Erasure Request (Web / Email)

If you wish to completely erase your Google account linkage, user profile, and all synchronized tracks from MapMe servers (including if you have uninstalled the app):

  1. Send an email to developer Tal Rosner at: talrosner@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: MapMe Data Deletion Request.
  3. In the body of the email, include the Google account email address associated with your MapMe profile.
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once your request is received.
✉️ Request Data Deletion via Email

2. What Data Is Deleted

Upon processing an account deletion request, the following data is permanently purged:

Data Category Specific Data Elements Deletion Action
User Profile Google account email, display name, photo URL, and Firebase Auth UID Permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication
Exploration Tracks All recorded walking, running, and driving sessions (distances, durations, speeds, timestamps) Permanently purged from Cloud Firestore
GPS Waypoints All geographical coordinate breadcrumbs (latitude, longitude, altitude) Permanently purged from Cloud Firestore
Points of Interest (POIs) Custom waypoint markers, descriptions, notes, and attached photos Permanently purged from cloud storage and Firestore
Aggregated Statistics Total lifetime distances, track counts, and weekly activity chart entries Permanently deleted

3. Data Retention and Timeline

Timeline & Retention:
  • Processing Time: All deletion requests submitted via email are processed within 30 days of verification.
  • Data Retention Post-Deletion: Zero. Once deleted, no backups or personal data of any kind are retained on our servers.
  • Guest / Anonymous Users: Guest sessions store data primarily on the local device sandbox. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage in Android Settings immediately erases all guest data.

4. Revoking Google Account Permissions

If you signed in with your Google account, you can also disconnect MapMe's access directly from your Google Security Settings at any time:

  1. Go to your Google Account Third-Party Connections page.
  2. Locate MapMe in the list of connected apps.
  3. Click Delete all connections that you have with MapMe.
